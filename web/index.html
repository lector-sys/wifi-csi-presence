What this actually measures. It detects general disturbance in the WiFi signal between one ESP32 and your router, which correlates with movement nearby. It cannot identify who or what caused the disturbance, and it derives no size, shape, height, position or identity of anything. A fan, a pet, a washing machine, a door swinging or the router retuning its rate can all move the number.
  • A single node on a single router link is noticeably noisier and less spatially resolved than a two-node setup — treat readings as a rough signal, not a measurement.
  • The "brief" / "sustained" label is a soft hint about how continuous the disturbance was. It is not a classification of the cause.
  • The figure below is decoration driven by one number. It is not an image, a pose, or a position.
  • Not for security, safety, medical or any critical use.
